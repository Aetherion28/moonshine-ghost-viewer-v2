# Moonshine V2.3.1 — focused test sheet

Please include your game version (JP, US or PAL) and whether you use Wii,
Wii U or Dolphin. Try the parts you normally use; nobody needs to repeat
every check on every game version.

## What matters most

- [ ] **File select:** with Intro Skip enabled, open the Moonshine menu on
  the A/B/C screen. Your custom appearance should work there, then remain
  correct after choosing a file.
- [ ] **Savestate crash:** make a new state, then use Save and Load during a
  short practice session. Turn the input display off and back on from the
  menu. Delfino Plaza and Pinna Park are useful locations if you already practice
  there. Report any freeze, broken video or crash, and keep the new crash
  files. Use states made in this build.
- [ ] **Live free camera:** turn free camera On, then choose **Resume
  gameplay** on that page. The world should move while you control the
  camera. Pause it again and advance a frame. Turning camera Off should
  restore the normal view without changing whether the game is paused.
  Also try Resume after opening free camera from Sunshine's pause screen.
  If you are recording a TAS, its frame count should keep moving in live freecam.
- [ ] **Camera movement:** slowly circle each stick and try shallow diagonal
  angles. Movement and looking should follow the stick smoothly. Check your
  preferred speed, look sensitivity and X boost; letting go should stop it.
- [ ] **Colour editor:** your old colours should look the same after updating.
  Edit one Mario/FLUDD part and one timer or overlay using Hue, Saturation and
  Lightness. Try white or black, then bring the colour back. Check that Keep
  saves the edit and Discard restores the old colour. Hold Y for finer steps.
- [ ] **Sunshine timer:** changing its colnoour should keep whichever
  Original/Custom appearance you selected. A short normal attempt should
  still time correctly, and Step should advance the QFT one frame.
- [ ] **Ghost message:** if you use a retained challenger, restart your level
  a couple of times. The challenger should stay available without repeating
  the ready message on every reset.
- [ ] **Usual practice:** play one ordinary IL, check its finish/PB, and load
  one new checkpoint or TAS project if those are part of your usual setup.
- [ ] **Japanese download:** check the new HSL labels and free-camera help
  for readability. The English download should stay English on JP too.

## If something goes wrong

Say what you pressed, what you expected, and what happened. A short video
helps with controls. For a crash, send the matching `.txt`, `.bin` and `.core`
files from the SD card, plus the game version and level. Please keep your
old states or projects rather than deleting them to test an update.
