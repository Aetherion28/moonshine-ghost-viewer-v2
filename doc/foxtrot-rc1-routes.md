# Moonshine FOXTROT RC1 — route assignments and checkpoints

**V2.3.0 candidate · Build E4DE048F**

Use this beside **RC1_TESTING.md**. It lists all **132 IL routes**, including older routes and the new handmade checkpoints.

**One pass per assigned route. Divide the courses between runners.** Put your name and region in Tester; write Pass, the problem, or Not tested in Result. Do not repeat a passed route just to fill another section. A Full Reds run can cover its internal red-coin sequence too: mark that Reds row “Covered in Full Reds”. That does not check starting the separate Reds IL, so share a few standalone Reds starts between runners as well.

Start each route from **Runs > ILs**, and enable **Runs > Timer and splits > Level splits**. Follow the checkpoints from left to right. Each should split once; the next checkpoint must not fill in a missed earlier one. Finish the route and check its result and split times. Turn off practice assistance for this route pass; the separate RC1 log covers TAS runs.

**Finish** means the route's normal finish, usually collecting its Shine. Entry segments finish when you enter the destination; Bianco Plant ends on its last hit and Honey Skip ends on death. “Finish only” means there are deliberately no intermediate checkpoints, not that the route is missing. Delfino Cop Secret still starts inside.

Talk checkpoints happen when dialogue starts, not when an NPC becomes talkable. Button checkpoints happen on the press. “Shine appears” is separate from collecting it. Coin, balloon, mirror and enemy numbers are running totals. For the seven main-course 100-coin routes, the 100th coin and the Finish are separate checkpoints; Delfino 100 has only its Finish.

Some movement checkpoints use position boundaries. The short X/Y/Z notes keep those boundaries precise; **Y is height**. They are included where there is no agreed landmark name. If you cannot identify one, mark it Not tested and describe where you got stuck.

The episode in a 100-coin row is its default. If you change a supported IL's starting episode with Z, write the chosen episode in Result. Share the episode-choice checks across a 100-coin route, a Hidden route and a Full Reds route, including Pinna or Sirena's extra area transitions.

## Priority assignments

Assign these once across the group, using the matching course row below for the result:

- **Pinna 1 — normal movies:** one runner watches the opening and ending movies. Talk, all four Mecha-Bowser hits and the Finish must still count.
- **Pinna 1 — Exit Area skips:** another runner uses Exit Area before Talk and after the fourth hit. The same checkpoints and final result must survive. These are two paths of one route; note which path you tested.
- **Gelato 5:** dialogue must create the Talk checkpoint.
- **Sirena 2 Reds / Noki 6 Reds:** the button must split immediately, before red coins.
- **Noki Hidden and the Plaza Shine-spawn routes:** check the Shine-appearance checkpoint as well as the Finish.
- **Ricco 8:** divide the Yoshi and Rocket approaches between two volunteers; either supplies the same single checkpoint.
