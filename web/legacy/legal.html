No Nintendo assets or binaries

This site does not host, contain or distribute any copyrighted Nintendo assets or binaries. That includes the game's executable, models, textures, animations, sounds, music, text and disc images.

Every piece of game data the viewer uses is read in your own browser from a copy of Super Mario Sunshine that you own and supply yourself, either as a disc image or as an extracted game folder. Nothing you load is uploaded to us or anyone else. Without your own copy, the viewer shows nothing from the game.

Independent reimplementation

The game logic that replays ghosts is an independent reimplementation, written for interoperability, research and the speedrunning community's practice tools. It is not a copy of Nintendo's code and is not a way to play the game without owning it.

Privacy

The site has no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads. What the viewer keeps, it keeps in your own browser: your settings, the stages it has already converted (so they load faster next time) and, where the browser allows it, a copy of your disc image, so the next visit opens it without asking. Forget disc removes that copy; clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything.

The page and its files come from GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The shared ghosts list and its downloads come from Delfino Ghosts' server, which sees your IP address when the list loads. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
